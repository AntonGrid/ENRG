# Epilogue

This book began with a question:

> *"Why does the energy produced by people have almost no digital representation of its own?"*

It ends with another question:

> *"How do we build an open language of trust between the physical and digital worlds?"*

Between these two questions lies an entire journey.

From idea to protocol.

From protocol to architecture.

From architecture to community.

Axis was not created in a single day.

It was created as an answer to a fundamental problem.

**How can the digital world honestly trust the physical?**

This problem will always remain relevant.

Technologies will change.

Devices will become different.

New algorithms will appear.

But trust will always remain the foundation of any system.

That is why Axis is not a final product.

It is a beginning.

The beginning of an open language of trust.

The beginning of a protocol that may one day become as natural as HTTP or TCP/IP.

If that happens, the goal will have been achieved.

Not because the author was right.

But because thousands of engineers around the world were able to build something greater on this foundation.

That is the true meaning of open protocols.

They outlive their own implementations.

They outlive companies.

Sometimes they outlive their creators.

That is why this book was written.

Not to record the history of one project.

But to preserve the story of an idea being born.

And perhaps to inspire those who will one day build something greater on this foundation than its author could have imagined.
